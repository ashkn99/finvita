About Finvita

Started by a fellow aquarium keeper who got tired of piecing together conflicting advice from a dozen different forums every time something looked off in the tank. The goal is simple: ask a few questions, point to the most likely cause, and suggest the products people actually use to fix it.

One person, no lab
Built and maintained solo, not by a company or a team of vets.
Common fixes only
Widely-used products for each issue, not the priciest option.
Always disclosed
Amazon Associates commission only, never a paid placement.

This is a growing, one-person project, not a veterinary or professional aquatic service. See our privacy policy, our affiliate disclosure, and the FAQ on the home page for more on what that does and doesn't mean.

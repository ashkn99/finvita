About Finvita

Finvita (Aquarium Health Helper) was started by a fellow aquarium keeper who got tired of piecing together conflicting advice from a dozen different forums every time something looked off in the tank. The goal is simple: ask a few questions, point to the most likely cause, and suggest the products people actually use to fix it.

Recommendations reflect common, widely-used products for each issue — not the most expensive option, and not a paid placement beyond the standard Amazon Associates commission described in our disclosure. This is a growing, one-person project, not a veterinary or professional aquatic service — see our privacy policy and the FAQ on the home page for more on what that does and doesn't mean.

Privacy Policy

This site does not have user accounts and does not collect any personal information directly. There is no sign-up, no tracking of who you are, and nothing you type into the diagnostic tool is saved or sent anywhere.

We do not currently use any third-party advertising networks. If that changes, this page will be updated to reflect it.
